Small Vosk models from https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models
Apache-2.0. The licence text is LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt in this folder.

The page loads one archive, only for the language being practised.
Each tar.gz is the Alpha Cephei zip repacked, same files, one top folder,
which is the layout vosk-browser reads. Nothing is moved between folders.
The page checks the tar.gz SHA-256 before it loads the archive.
The recogniser is vosk-browser 0.0.8, also Apache-2.0.
